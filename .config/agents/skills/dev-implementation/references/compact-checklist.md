# Compact implementation checklist

Apply every item before accepting compact completion:

1. Bind the invoking agent as controller, approved intent, acceptance IDs, one exact child owner, owned paths/surfaces, permitted effects, applicable project instructions, and the child's concrete receiver identity. Use a planless direct contract unless an already-approved lean plan is required by dependency or recovery needs. Standalone and router entry both use the invoking controller in place; a separate controller requires an explicitly approved topology.
2. Confirm the work is settled, reversible, one-lineage, free of independent-proof requirements, and covered by deterministic direct checks. Any material ambiguity, destructive/external effect, fan-in, or independent-assurance need disqualifies compact.
3. Dispatch all code-changing work to the child. The controller validates and schedules only. Missing transport capability that cannot preserve required ownership or same-child follow-up is `transport-unavailable`; a concrete failed invocation is assessed under the shared recovery policy before escalation. The controller never downgrades, substitutes ownership, or recursively delegates another controller.
   OMP collectors with `taskDepth > 0` stop `transport-unavailable` before child
   allocation, without delegated-controller substitution. Depth 0 still needs
   all required capabilities; other capable hosts and named lifecycle
   consumers retain their own semantics.
4. Preflight an explicit caller-selected response-object schema, resumable same-child transport and exact controller/child/launch-job/task/attempt/`candidate` bindings. The child terminal-completes its type-absent candidate before final smoke or Handoff, without incremental publication or parking. Retain the exact original launch result and row, validate and decode the adapter-designated data, and admit exact candidate identity. Then bind child/controller/task/attempt/operation/logical-Handoff/phase/schema/invocation and send `~/.agents/references/impl-rethink/impl-rethink.md` through the portable host-selected seam. This exact collection needs no additional consent, attendance, external-supervisor or abort-capability preflight. The child applies code then test rethink, at most one correction, all direct checks and changed-path smoke, and returns one lean Handoff. On OMP use `write agent://<child>`, one outstanding request, then a terminal type-absent yield with exactly one string `response` field. Retain the first subsequent task-job result and row for that child before decoding; require matching `agentUrlId`, successful resolution, valid inherited caller schema and exact report identity once. Earlier jobs must already be retained. Bind by child/request order, never job-ID equality or novelty; held IDs may be suffixed and evicted IDs reused without waiting. Delivery receipts, old/foreign/duplicate rows, text-only or rejected results and relays are not replies; failed delivery, changed revived identity, observed no-job registration failure or the no-job wait stop ends that request without resend/replacement. OMP has no token/restatement fallback. Other hosts with native correlation keep the portable token/message rules; neither capability means `transport-unavailable`. Job settlement is neither task completion nor disposal.
   On OMP every terminal child return, launch or follow-up, is one direct
   native `yield` tool call, never through eval, `tool.yield`,
   `getattr(tool, 'yield')`, a prelude helper or another tool bridge: a bridged
   yield reports `Result submitted.` but registers no launch or wake job. Every
   controller request to the child states this rule. Keep native wait active
   in the same controller turn from the launch or eligible follow-up receipt
   until the original matching result and row are retained; ordinary wait
   messages, including `wakeRelay` notices, do not finish collection. Do not
   end that turn or admit display-only auto-delivery. Read follow-up outcomes
   from `details.message.receipts[].outcome`: `failed` and `injected` stop
   without waiting for a new row, as does changed-identity `revived`. Only
   `woken` or same-identity `revived` enters collection. After reading that
   receipt, a later native `wait` result with empty `details.jobs` and text
   `No running background jobs to wait for.` is the adapter's no-job wait stop:
   stop that request as a missing reply without admission or further wait and
   assess it under the shared execution-recovery policy. Absence of a row
   alone proves nothing. No resend, replacement, reset, polling rule or
   alternate-source recovery.
5. If execution machinery fails or would otherwise cause an execution-related blocker, keep the same execution owner and assess `skill://dev-implementation/references/execution-recovery.md` before escalation. Explicitly apply its recovery rethink only before an eligible retry, retain failed evidence, cause, and allowance in the lean Handoff, and keep this separate from the single implementation rethink and semantic attempts. Collection of an already-authorized recovery return from the same bound child uses the same exact role-and-purpose preflight exemption; it gains no second implementation rethink, deadline, observer, replay, or replacement. Custom collectors retain their own preflight.
6. Apply `skill://dev-implementation/references/test-value.md` as the sole permanent-test policy for every changed or proposed permanent test. Do not inspect unrelated portfolio tests.
7. After the completed Handoff, the same child loads `papercut` once; the controller substitutes only if that child is unavailable.
8. End the route only after the remaining approved outcome completes. Compact dispatches no independent reviewer, verifier, learning assessment, audit, or second implementation self-rethink. An in-place controller consumes the child return and finishes without a self-Handoff; an approved delegated controller alone returns across its real boundary to the concrete route owner. A failed check stops only after the shared policy identifies no permitted execution recovery; unavailable required ownership and explicit exhausted limits remain stops.
