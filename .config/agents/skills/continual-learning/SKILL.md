# Continual Learning

## Modes and scheduling

Use exactly one explicit mode:

- `assess`: assess one settled outcome from the lean intake below.
- `review`: inspect only a human-pointed current session, path, or ID and return proposals or missing evidence without mutation.
- `deep`: investigate and curate only under separate explicit authority for severe, recurring, cross-contract, stale, or conflicting guidance.

The lifecycle caller owns scheduling. This skill never schedules itself or another mode. When a specialty adapter owns an assessment, only that adapter may invoke this portable body. For engineering, `dev-continual-learning` invokes `assess` once after standard or high review and verification; compact skips it. A bare `review`, session title, elapsed time, count, artifact volume, score, or unchanged Handoff selects nothing.

## Lean assess intake

Require:

- one settled outcome;
- its affected paths;
- its lean Handoffs in caller order;
- every papercut result from the bounded outcome; and
- every available complete Learning Candidate.

Zero complete candidates remains eligible and normally returns `no durable learning`. Keep incomplete candidates as named evidence with their missing fields. Do not require or accept workflow manifests, digests, counters, receipts, role slots, retry identities, archive locators, or transcript summaries. Do not inspect unrelated artifacts to fill missing intake.

## Candidate qualification

A complete candidate states:

- the proposed durable guidance or correction;
- exact source evidence and affected project scope;
- the existing authorized destination;
- recurrence or independently verified severity;
- how the statement prevents recurrence;
- sensitivity and redaction handling;
- conflicts, overlap, or supersession; and
- a direct source case plus an independent adjacent or near-miss check.

One explicit durable correction or verified stable project fact may qualify from one settled occurrence. Ordinary process guidance requires two independent settled outcomes. One severe independently verified safety, data-loss, authority, expensive-failure, or high-impact incident may qualify when the proposed statement directly prevents recurrence.

Reject transient state, guesses, secrets, personal data, raw transcripts, unverified claims, unsettled hypotheses, generic advice, provider trivia, and disguised product, architecture, scope, compatibility, destructive, or shipping decisions. A candidate is evidence, not mutation authority.

Use the narrowest existing project-owned destination already authorized for the statement. Update, merge, or remove before adding. Do not create a new skill, rule, domain artifact, state service, memory service, or hidden authority without explicit human approval. Cross-repository user guidance is human-managed and remains a Deep proposal unless separately authorized.

A papercut-originated candidate carries its unchanged `PC-ID`; a missing or mismatched ID leaves settlement open. This skill never reads or writes a papercut ledger.

## Curation

1. Bind the lean intake and account for every complete and incomplete candidate. Do not change the settled implementation or rerun its assurance.
2. Inspect only the affected evidence and candidate destinations. Check qualification, authority, scope, redaction, conflicts, and the prevention relationship.
3. Prefer the least-specific sufficient statement and the existing destination that covers the source case without capturing the independent adjacent case.
4. Reread the destination immediately before mutation. Preserve unrelated work and decline mutation unless the exact learning delta can be safely restored.
5. Make the smallest authorized update, then run the candidate's source, adjacent, syntax, registration, precedence, duplication, and staleness checks. Use a fresh independent read-only evaluator when semantic judgment is part of the declared check.
6. If validation fails, restore only the learning delta and report the failure. Do not retry, weaken checks, broaden authority, or attempt another candidate mutation to manufacture success.
7. Return one terminal assessment. Do not recursively curate the curator edit.

## Terminal assessment

Return exactly one status:

- `curated` when at least one authorized durable change passes its complete checks;
- `no durable learning` when no candidate qualifies or every candidate is safely rejected or deferred without a durable change; or
- `blocked <reason>` when intake, authority, mutation, or validation cannot be completed safely.

Account compactly for guidance changes, checks, skipped candidates, and each originating `PC-ID` disposition. Papercut dispositions are proposals to the caller: candidate-specific durable correction may be `fixed`, final candidate rejection may be `rejected`, replacement may be `superseded`, and incomplete, blocked, deferred, global, or unrelated outcomes remain `open`.

Every invocation is terminal. Do not retry, resume, reinvoke, or add another curator for the same outcome. An ordinary `blocked <reason>` is a residual risk and does not invalidate the settled implementation. The only completion-blocking result is a directly established conflict in a current governing rule that makes that implementation invalid or unsafe; name the rule, contradiction, and affected behavior. Do not construct completion input for that conflict.

## Portability and boundaries

Run this body without repository ADRs, workflow documents, transcripts, JSONL history, scores, evaluation or learning ledgers, long-term memory, or host adapters. Hosts may invoke it differently, but mode eligibility, qualification, curation, statuses, and stops are the same.

Do not create a task or Handoff, dispatch another owner, repair implementation, settle a papercut record, infer completion, present output, or ship. Return the assessment to the invoking adapter or direct caller; that owner handles its existing lifecycle and completion.
