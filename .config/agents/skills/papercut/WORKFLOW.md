# Papercut workflow

## Human overview

Papercut records small, reusable friction in repository-owned work. Candidate-triggered activation remains available throughout current dev, product, custom, and direct work. After every work-task Common Handoff, one soft look may qualify at most one current candidate. An initialized repository can record it automatically; an uninitialized or read-only repository receives a disclosed report-only result. No candidate means no skill or ledger access and no papercut output. Repositories opt in explicitly through `papercut init` or an exact approved `init-ask` effect. Durable guidance still uses its existing workflow and assurance.
Within the seven-event session envelope, papercut retains only its existing `Handoff → papercut look/skip` position. Portable `continual-learning` owns eligible assessment semantics, and a specialty sends normalized terminal accounting to `completion-presentation` only after its existing validation and exact-record settlement. This projection creates no eager loading, papercut stage, second Handoff, or ledger access by the presenter.

## Module design

The always-applied rule notices candidates during current work and takes one soft look after each work-task Common Handoff. `SKILL.md` owns qualification, redaction, semantic record selection, Learning Candidate construction, routing, exact workflow-result mapping, and disclosure. `scripts/papercut_ledger.py` is private mechanics with four operations: `init`, `list`, `record`, and `resolve`. The repository ledger stores compact v2 evidence only. `init-ask` may delegate one approved initialization call but owns no papercut semantics or storage. Papercut is never a task, Methods token, workflow or route stage, todo phase, assurance event, or per-task continual-learning trigger; capture changes no task state. No host adapter, setup registry, queue, or second papercut owner exists.

## Current flow

1. A current candidate may qualify while dev, product, custom, or direct work is active.
2. A work task emits its Common Handoff before the always-applied rule takes its one soft look. That look may qualify at most one current candidate; without one it performs no skill or ledger access and emits no papercut output.
3. The skill rejects owned, noisy, or sensitive cases; redacts one candidate; and checks exact authority without changing task state or dispatching learning.
4. With initialized writable storage, `record` creates, deduplicates, updates, or reopens one stable record. Otherwise the skill reports without mutation.
5. Independent evidence may form one complete Learning Candidate with an immutable originating `PC-ID`; the current specialty adapter or direct lifecycle owner freezes its evidence and invokes portable `continual-learning` when eligible.
6. The portable owner preserves that `PC-ID` and returns one candidate-specific result. The dev backend, product router, custom owner, or direct-response owner then performs at most one settlement call through the same portable seam. After settlement and specialty completion validation, `completion-presentation` reports the normalized papercut result exactly once without invoking papercut or accessing the ledger.
7. Durable correction maps to `fixed`, candidate-specific rejection to `rejected`, and replacement to `superseded`. Blocked, incomplete, deferred, broad, global, or unrelated outcomes remain open.
8. Resolution removes current observation prose. A later distinct recurrence reopens the same stable record and retains its latest resolution.

## Included, excluded, and deferred

Included: candidate-triggered current-work capture across dev, product, custom, and direct work; one soft look after each work-task Common Handoff; explicit opt-in through `papercut` or approved `init-ask`; redaction; semantic deduplication; compact evidence; proposal-only review; immutable candidate delivery; exact-record settlement; OMP/Grok portable invocation.

Excluded: task or plan state; Methods; workflow, route, todo, assurance, or continual-learning stages; per-task learning dispatch; transcript/history/memory mining; counts or calendars as proof; background jobs; tracker or repair state; product, domain, or ADR authority; staging, shipping, or external effects; automatic repository initialization.

Deferred: generic repository agent memory and any new supported setup integration without an approved owner and observable seam.

## Authority and maintenance

ADR-0007 D24 governs this module. ADR-0004 D07 owns generic engineering assessment qualification and the thin dev adapter projection. ADR-0009 D27 owns portable continual-learning modes, the seven-event envelope, and generic completed presentation. ADR-0008 D25 owns the separate repository setup interface. Product P07 and custom workflow owners retain their outcomes; papercut settlement cannot create or alter them. The ledger is evidence, never authority. When changing this module, synchronize the rule, skill, evals, workflow documentation, and current ADR projection; recheck the helper and update it only when its private mechanics change. Preserve the exact four-mode/four-operation interfaces and verify clean removal of v1 command and sidecar contracts.
