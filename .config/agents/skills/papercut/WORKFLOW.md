# Papercut workflow

## Human overview

Papercut records small, reusable friction in repository-owned work. Candidate-triggered activation remains available throughout current dev, product, custom, and direct work. After every work-task Common Handoff, the same work child performs one soft look; the root falls back only when that child is unavailable. The look begins with the completed attempt and widens to its bounded execution lineage only when directly referenced structured evidence exposes a severe or recurring escalation marker. It may classify stable root-cause candidates but selects at most one for automatic capture. A clean final attempt cannot erase severe structured friction from earlier attempts. An initialized repository can record the selected candidate automatically; an uninitialized or read-only repository receives a disclosed report-only result. No candidate means no skill or ledger access and no papercut output. Repositories opt in explicitly through `papercut init` or an exact approved `init-ask` effect. Durable guidance still uses its existing workflow and assurance.
Within the seven-event session envelope, papercut retains only its existing `Handoff → papercut look/skip` position. Portable `continual-learning` owns eligible assessment semantics, and a specialty retains every look result or none-only accounting item in deterministic work-Handoff order. It sends only ordered material results to `completion-presentation` after existing validation and exact-record settlement. This projection creates no eager loading, papercut stage, second Handoff, or ledger access by the presenter.

## Module design

The always-applied rule notices candidates during current work and owns the bounded structured-lineage activation gate. It requires the same work child to take one soft look after each work-task Common Handoff; only child unavailability permits root fallback. `SKILL.md` owns qualification, the allowed lineage sources, repository-ownership judgment, redaction, stable root-cause consolidation, semantic record selection, Learning Candidate construction, routing, exact workflow-result mapping, and disclosure. `scripts/papercut_ledger.py` remains private mechanics with four operations: `init`, `list`, `record`, and `resolve`; its stable identity remains `surface` plus `summary`. The repository ledger stores compact v2 evidence only. `init-ask` may delegate one approved initialization call but owns no papercut semantics or storage. Papercut is never a task, Methods token, workflow or route stage, todo phase, assurance event, or per-task continual-learning trigger; capture changes no task state. No host adapter, retry ledger, per-attempt record, setup registry, queue, or second papercut envelope exists.

## Current flow

1. A current candidate may qualify while dev, product, custom, or direct work is active.
2. A work task emits its Common Handoff before the same work child takes its one soft look; the root performs it only if that child is unavailable. The look starts with the completed attempt. It widens only when the directly referenced current plan blocker table, same-lineage Common Handoffs, or immutable attempt receipts expose an exhausted attempt or repair budget, a recurring blocker class, loss of a completed result before persistence, or repeated human authority needed solely for execution mechanics. Without a marker it performs no lineage inspection.
3. Markers permit inspection but do not prove reusable friction. A repository-owned final accounting step that considered only terminal observations despite a marker may itself qualify as run-lineage blindness. The skill otherwise applies the unchanged repository-owned and reusable-friction bar, consolidates repeated symptoms by stable surface and root-cause class, omits volatile receipt metadata from identity and summary, and selects at most one candidate for automatic capture. Other stable root-cause candidates receive an explicit report-only qualification or ownership rejection in the same compact result, without a second ledger call.
4. Without any qualifying candidate the look performs no skill or ledger access and emits no papercut output, while the workflow still accounts for the completed look. A clean final attempt does not supersede severe qualifying structured evidence from earlier attempts.
5. With initialized writable storage, `record` creates, deduplicates, updates, or reopens the one selected stable record. Otherwise the skill reports it without mutation.
6. Independent evidence may form one complete Learning Candidate with an immutable originating `PC-ID`; the current specialty adapter or direct lifecycle owner freezes its evidence and invokes portable `continual-learning` when eligible.
7. The portable owner preserves that `PC-ID` and returns one candidate-specific result. The dev backend, product router, custom owner, or direct-response owner then performs at most one settlement call through the same portable seam. After settlement and specialty completion validation, `completion-presentation` reports the normalized papercut result exactly once without invoking papercut or accessing the ledger.
8. Durable correction maps to `fixed`, candidate-specific rejection to `rejected`, and replacement to `superseded`. Blocked, incomplete, deferred, broad, global, or unrelated outcomes remain open. Resolution removes current observation prose; a later distinct recurrence reopens the same stable record and retains its latest resolution.

## Included, excluded, and deferred

Included: candidate-triggered current-work capture across dev, product, custom, and direct work; one soft look after each work-task Common Handoff; bounded escalation through directly referenced plan blocker tables, same-lineage Common Handoffs, and immutable attempt receipts; stable surface/root-cause consolidation; explicit repository-ownership rationale; one selected automatic capture; explicit opt-in through `papercut` or approved `init-ask`; redaction; semantic deduplication; compact evidence; proposal-only review; immutable candidate delivery; exact-record settlement; OMP/Grok portable invocation.

Excluded: task or plan state as identity; Methods; workflow, route, todo, assurance, or continual-learning stages; per-task learning dispatch; raw transcript, history, memory, provider-log, repository-wide, or session-wide mining; counts, exhausted budgets, authority revisions, attempt IDs, models, providers, paths, hashes, or timestamps as qualification proof or stable identity; one record per attempt; ordinary assertion failures; external provider or runtime behavior without a repository-owned prevention seam; intentional boundaries; concurrent or unattributed activity; harmless acknowledgements; one-off noise; background jobs; tracker or repair state; product, domain, or ADR authority; staging, shipping, or external effects; automatic repository initialization.

Deferred: generic repository agent memory and any new supported setup integration without an approved owner and observable seam.

## Authority and maintenance

ADR-0007 D24 governs this module. ADR-0004 D07 owns generic engineering assessment qualification and the thin dev adapter projection. ADR-0009 D27 owns portable continual-learning modes, the seven-event envelope, and generic completed presentation. ADR-0008 D25 owns the separate repository setup interface. Product P07 and custom workflow owners retain their outcomes; papercut settlement cannot create or alter them. The ledger is evidence, never authority. When changing this module, synchronize the rule, skill, evals, workflow documentation, and current ADR projection; recheck the helper and update it only when its private mechanics change. Preserve the exact four-mode/four-operation interfaces, one automatic record call per look, and clean removal of v1 command and sidecar contracts.
