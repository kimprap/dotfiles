# Human-facing language

For human replies, questions, approval screens, completion, and artifacts explicitly marked human-only, prefer familiar concise terms and keep necessary technical detail precise.

Do not simplify or rewrite internal plans, IDs, schemas, code, tool output, or agent transport. Those surfaces retain their exact technical language.
