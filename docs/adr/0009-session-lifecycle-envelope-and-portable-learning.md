# Session lifecycle envelope and portable workflow owners

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Date:** 2026-08-20  
**Updated:** 2026-08-25
**Decision IDs:** D27  
**Related authority:** ADR-0001 D01, D13, D15, D18, D19, D26; ADR-0003 D03, D04, D22; ADR-0004 D07, D23; ADR-0005 P01; ADR-0007 D24

## Scope

This decision governs one host-neutral session envelope, portable continual-learning semantics, and the sole generic completion presenter. It applies to supported engineering, product, custom, and direct work after each specialty has validated its own outcome. It preserves specialty internals, one existing specialty Handoff, exact-manifest assurance gates, papercut capture and settlement ownership, and the existing engineering assurance tail. It does not create runtime state, approve effects, decide specialty completion, or authorize delivery.

## Context / problem

Engineering continual learning and final completion rendering were embedded in engineering-specific owners. Product and custom workflows could reuse the intended semantics only by copying them or by routing through an unrelated engineering owner. Pause and compaction also needed an explicit portable boundary: callers must preserve their own completed stages, selected role slot, counters, and artifact identities without promoting transcripts, plans, ADRs, or a new state service into runtime authority.

The framework needs one small outer envelope while each specialty remains opaque. Learning semantics must run from the same portable body in OMP and Grok. Completed outcomes need one concise, specialty-neutral rendering, but completion validity, papercut settlement, assurance, and product authority must stay with their existing owners.

## Decisions

### D27 — Session lifecycle envelope and portable workflow owners

- **Scope:** the seven-event session envelope; portable `assess`, `review`, and `deep` continual-learning modes; exact-manifest re-entry and duplicate-dispatch boundaries; one generic completed-outcome presenter with a fixed current fenced input, filled evidence, and durable continuation; specialty normalization; papercut ordering; portable distribution; and deferred future intent.
- **Decision:** Use exactly `intake → classify → work-specialty → Handoff → papercut look (or silent skip) → assess (or silent skip) → present`. A specialty's internal lifecycle remains opaque. Repair, reapproval, user promotion, explicit review, shipping, pause, compaction, and other re-entry are events against the current envelope, not stages, nested orchestration, or a persisted DAG. The envelope stores nothing. `present` is a terminal projection of one already-settled Handoff, not a task, approval, transition, state, dispatchable owner, worker, or second Handoff.
- **Decision:** Before any specialty role dispatch or completion decision, the specialty adapter compares current bytes with its once-bound target and applicable-rule manifests. Pause or compaction restores the same specialty-owned completed stages, selected role slot, attempt, repair, review, and learning counters, terminal state, and digest-bound artifact and receipt identities. Missing, stale, or contradictory references block rather than reopen completed work. Repeating an already-issued or consumed tuple of parent outcome, target-manifest digest, rule-manifest digest, role slot, and semantic-attempt or grant identity blocks before dispatch as `idempotency-violation` and consumes nothing. A distinct authorized slot and a recorded pre-semantic safe transport retry keep their existing eligibility.
- **Decision:** `continual-learning` owns three portable modes. `assess` accepts a settled eligible outcome, its completed Handoff, and a nonempty affected-artifact manifest. It forwards every complete Learning Candidate and keeps incomplete candidates as evidence. A nonempty affected-artifact outcome with no complete candidate may return `NO DURABLE LEARNING`; no affected artifact and no complete candidate is empty and causes a silent skip. `review` requires an explicit human pointer to `this session`, one path, or one ID, reads only that evidence, and returns proposals or missing evidence without mutation or dispatch. `deep` requires an explicit human request or settled severe, recurring, cross-contract, stale, or conflicting evidence and remains separately authorized unless an exact current contradiction blocks correctness. A title, name, count, timer, calendar, artifact volume, unchanged Handoff, score, background mining, or transcript corpus never qualifies a mode. The bare word `review` never selects between continual-learning review, `dev-code-review`, and papercut review.
- **Decision:** When a specialty adapter already owns an outcome's assessment tail, that adapter is the only path for the outcome. Otherwise, one explicit portable mode invocation is valid when its mode-specific eligibility holds. The envelope never auto-dispatches any mode. Engineering keeps route-visible owner `dev-continual-learning`, which invokes portable `assess` once inside the existing standard or high-consequence tail task and invokes portable `deep` only on a separately authorized engineering Deep route. Compact invokes neither owner.
- **Decision:** Preserve the reporter-owned mutation tuple exactly: `id; candidate_revision`; source identity, revision, expected, proof, and baseline; adjacent identity, revision, expected, proof, independence, and `baseline=fresh-required`; `proof_mode: deterministic | semantic | mixed`; and `semantic_evaluator: none | separate`. The lifecycle caller validates authority, freshness, completeness, adjacent independence, and proof classification; canonicalizes sorted-key compact UTF-8 JSON; and binds one `CE-` identity to the lowercase SHA-256 digest before mutation. The portable curator cannot create, replace, weaken, or omit that binding. Matching source evidence may be reused while its target, environment, expectation, and proof method remain exact; the adjacent baseline is always fresh; both rerun afterward. Deterministic proof uses no second evaluator. Each semantic facet uses one fresh read-only non-curator `PASS | FAIL | FLAKY | INCONCLUSIVE` result bound to the tuple. Complete proof permits `CURATED`; a stable safe deterministic or semantic failure restores only the curator delta and returns `NO DURABLE LEARNING`; missing, stale, tampered, flaky, inconclusive, or unsafe proof returns `BLOCKED`.
- **Decision:** Continual learning returns exactly `CURATED | NO DURABLE LEARNING | BLOCKED` with exactly `Updated`, `Added`, `Removed`, `Skipped`, `Validation`, `Deep candidate`, and `Papercut outcome`. A complete papercut-originated candidate carries one unchanged originating `PC-ID`; the portable owner returns candidate-specific `fixed | rejected | superseded | open` mapping evidence but never reads or writes the ledger. The current specialty or session adapter alone may settle one exact terminal record after the work Handoff and papercut look. Incomplete, blocked, deferred, broad, global, unrelated, narrow-authority, and helper-failure results remain open.
- **Decision:** `completion-presentation` is the sole generic terminal renderer. It activates only when the same caller already holds exactly one current `completion-presentation-input` JSON fence with keys in this exact order: `status`, `outcome`, `changed`, `verification`, `papercut`, `learning`, `residual_risk`, `resume_from`, `handoff`, `constraints`, `next`. Status is exactly `completed`; Changed has one to three nonempty items; every other value is nonempty; every decoded string and Changed item excludes C0/C1 controls and Unicode line or paragraph separators; Constraints contains the exact clause `shipping not authorized` exactly once; and Next is `none` or one exact action and receiver already authorized by the current specialty Handoff. Unknown, duplicate, missing, reordered, stale, prior-turn, malformed, placeholder, line-breaking/control-bearing, durability-invalid, non-completed, learning-`BLOCKED`, or shipping-bearing input emits no generic completed report.
- **Decision:** The renderer mechanically preserves the accepted control-safe values and emits exactly `Completed`, `Evidence`, and `Continuation` sections. Outcome states the completed result; Changed names openable human or canonical artifacts; Verification names a check, terminal verdict, and fetchable immutable evidence; Papercut and Learning preserve their normalized machine values plus concise human meaning; Residual risk contains only current material uncertainty; Resume from is a durable openable locator with immutable revision or digest; Handoff is the existing portable immutable Common/Product Handoff locator or approved in-conversation form tied to Resume from, while an unresolved `local://` candidate is invalid; Constraints contains current boundaries including `shipping not authorized`; and Next preserves only the current specialty-authorized receiver action or `none`. `Papercut` and `Learning` appear exactly once. Completed `Route`, route history, an exposed input fence, preface, epilogue, injected structure, or extra heading is forbidden.
- **Decision:** Render Changed as a labeled nested list with one bullet per accepted Changed item in input order, including the single-item case. The current grammar has no other artifact-valued array; scalar Verification, Resume from, and Handoff locators remain inline.
- **Decision:** Specialty owners keep completion validity and normalization. `dev-ask` remains the engineering router and completion validator. `product-ask` remains the product router, Product Handoff validator, PRD and iteration owner, and papercut settlement owner. Custom and direct specialties validate their own terminal Handoffs. After current validation and settlement, the same caller creates one current fence, applies the presenter directly, and emits only the report. Non-success outcomes keep their specialty-specific reports. The presenter never decides completion, reruns or validates evidence, invokes skipped skills, accesses manifests, counters, receipts, Context Packs, backend attempts, or the papercut ledger, infers shipping, persists state, approves effects, creates a task, dispatch, child, transition, route completion, plan, workflow, or Handoff, or receives lifecycle authority.
- **Decision:** Both portable executable skills run without loading this repository's ADR or workflow documents. OMP and Grok use the same bodies; only invocation syntax differs. Consumer distribution consists of executable skills and activation rules, plus the existing optional `.agents/papercuts.json` seam and an optional repository guidance pointer. It does not copy this ADR corpus, add an `init-ask` catalog row, create a host adapter, or add a setup or lifecycle store.
- **Decision:** Portable distribution may include the exact manual shared package `surface-verification-adapter` and the disabled-from-model wrappers `create-surface-verification-adapter` and `maintain-surface-verification-adapter`. OMP `/skill:<name>` and Grok `/<name>` explicit invocation remain available, while ordinary model discovery is disabled. Repository-owned generated adapters are distributed only with their consuming repository, and no upstream alias, automatic activation rule, setup row, host-specific semantic fork, or lifecycle state is added.
- **Why:** One semantic owner per concern prevents dev-specific clones and completion-format drift. A non-persistent envelope exposes ordering without becoming orchestration. Caller-owned recovery and completion validity preserve existing assurance and specialty authority. One fixed fenced input makes activation deterministic, while filled evidence and durable continuation make the concise report useful to both the current human and a later owner.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** A dev-specific semantic clone forks qualification and curation. A dev presentation adapter or dispatchable presenter creates another owner without adding authority. A second Handoff obscures the existing recovery envelope. A persisted DAG, state machine, session ledger, evaluation ledger, learning ledger, presenter state, or score becomes competing runtime authority. Session titles, transcript or JSONL mining, background mining, automatic `rethink`, weakness scores, and count or timer triggers infer qualification from noise. Per-task learning duplicates the one specialty tail. Completed Route exposes prospective machinery after work is settled. Mutable or host-session-only resume references cannot support later continuation. ADR copies in consumer repositories fork authority. A new `init-ask` row or host-specific runtime exception creates another distribution path.
- **Consequences:** Supported specialties compose inside one seven-event envelope while retaining their internal lifecycles and one Handoff. Engineering keeps exactly one `dev-verification → dev-code-review → dev-continual-learning` tail, and the adapter invokes portable `assess` once. Product settlement still occurs before normalization and preserves its current engineering receiver in Next. A paused or compacted run resumes only from current digest-bound specialty references. Every valid generic completed report is built from one current fence, includes filled verification and durable Resume from/Handoff/Constraints evidence, and contains no Route. Blocks and other non-success outcomes remain specialty-specific. Portable eval registries own semantic examples; dev and product registries prove only their adapter and normalization integration.
- **Reopen when:** a specialty cannot normalize terminal evidence without moving its authority; two supported hosts require different semantics rather than transport syntax; a valid recovery case cannot be represented by existing specialty references; the fixed completed report omits material human evidence; portable distribution needs an additional authorized seam; or a proposed frontend or host runtime is separately specified.

## Verification expectations

- The only session order is `intake → classify → work-specialty → Handoff → papercut look/skip → assess/skip → present`; re-entry never adds a stage or persisted state.
- Portable assessment distinguishes nonempty affected work, zero complete candidates, incomplete candidate evidence, and empty or ineligible intake; review reads only explicit pointers; Deep uses only approved triggers.
- Mutation remains bound to one frozen reporter tuple, fresh adjacent proof, correct semantic evaluator ownership, safe restoration, three terminal outcomes, and the seven-field result.
- Exact duplicate dispatch tuples block before a role call or counter, slot, transition, or Handoff change; distinct authorized slots and recorded pre-semantic safe retries remain eligible.
- The presenter activates only from exactly one current control-safe eleven-key `completion-presentation-input` fence and emits exactly `Completed`, `Evidence`, and `Continuation`, exactly one Papercut line, exactly one Learning line, filled Verification and Residual risk, and Continuation fields for State, durable Resume from, existing Handoff, Constraints containing `shipping not authorized`, and specialty-authorized Next.
- Compact and standard registry outputs match their bound goldens; every valid report has one to three openable Changed artifacts, named verification with verdict and immutable fetchable evidence, normalized papercut and learning values, and no completed Route, exposed fence, injected structure, extra heading, or shipping-bearing Next.
- Every valid compact, standard, product, and custom golden renders Changed as a nested list with one bullet per input item, including one-item inputs; scalar locator fields remain inline.
- Missing, stale, prior-turn, duplicate, malformed, reordered, unknown/duplicate/missing-key, empty, placeholder, changed-cardinality, decoded CR/LF/NEL, Unicode-line-separator, C0/C1-control, durability-invalid, unresolved-local-Handoff, absolute-host-session-Handoff, constraint-invalid, unauthorized-Next, non-completed, and learning-`BLOCKED` input emits no generic completed report.
- OMP reads the repository skill and Grok discovers the project skill from the same portable body; host model renders match the fixed structure and field mapping without requiring whole-output byte equality.
- Engineering and product completion validation, assurance, papercut settlement, product authority, compact exclusion, exact Next authorization, and non-success reporting remain with their current owners; the same caller presents after fence construction, and the presenter receives no task, dispatch, state, approval, Context Pack, backend attempt, or Handoff.
