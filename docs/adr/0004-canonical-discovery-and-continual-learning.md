## Scope

This decision governs conditional discovery of the generic engineering workflow's current contracts and ADR registry, focused decision provenance, and Standard versus Deep continual-learning maintenance, including the frozen evaluation boundary for mutating curation. It applies to the repository-local `.agents/AGENTS.md` pointer, `docs/adr/INDEX.md`, active workflow ADRs, `dev-continual-learning`, the backend curation seam, and the current workflow reference. It does not make ADRs runtime state, require eager loading, authorize background curation, turn repository evidence into memory or authority, or permit mutation of `/Users/kim/.agents/AGENTS.md`.

## Context / problem

Repository-local guidance can be injected automatically, but workflow references and ADRs are not necessarily in ordinary agent context. Loading all workflow history for every task would waste context and give rejected or superseded records an opportunity to masquerade as executable policy. The generic workflow therefore uses one conditional repository pointer: agents changing or diagnosing it read `.config/agents/skills/dev-ask/WORKFLOW.md` and `docs/adr/INDEX.md`, while ordinary tasks read only their applicable skill/rule and the active ADRs named by their Task Contract. The index is a stable, human-readable, non-executable registry; active skills/rules and current `WORKFLOW.md` own current behavior, focused active ADRs own durable rationale, approved requirements/specifications/direct authority govern their concerns, and Atlas or external sources remain advisory. At completion, narrow evidence can justify a focused rule or contract repair, but invocation counters, calendar audits, transcript mining, or broad “self-improvement” claims would create hidden state and unbounded maintenance.

## Decisions

### D07 — Deep continual learning

- **Scope:** Portable `continual-learning` assess/review/deep semantics, the thin `dev-continual-learning` adapter, terminal backend ordering, maintenance boundaries, papercut settlement, and guidance specificity.
- **Decision:** After a settled standard or high-consequence engineering outcome, the one route-visible `dev-continual-learning` adapter invokes portable `continual-learning` mode `assess` exactly once on the current once-bound target and applicable-rule manifest. Compact never dispatches it. A mutating Learning Candidate discovered during compact work is deferred to separately approved noncompact maintenance.
- **Decision:** A numbered `dev-continual-learning` profile-tail row consumes the existing terminal assessment; when absent, the backend schedules that same adapter once after current-target verification and review. Learning never uses `worker-closure/v1`, consumes a work attempt or repair token, or repairs implementation.
- **Decision:** Every work child performs its papercut soft look only after sealing its Common Handoff; the root falls back only when the child is unavailable. Retain accounting in deterministic work-Handoff order. Papercut capture never triggers a learning assessment. A complete candidate may be supplied to the eventual assessor with its immutable `PC-ID`.
- **Decision:** The portable assessor remains sole owner of assess eligibility, qualification, affected-artifact scope, mutation procedure/proof, safe restoration, Deep eligibility, and terminal result semantics. After a terminal result, the backend may settle only the exact supplied papercut record: verified durable correction maps to `fixed`, candidate-specific rejection maps to `rejected`, replacement maps to `superseded`, and incomplete/deferred/non-specific results keep it open.
- **Decision:** Portable `assess` may maintain an existing repository-owned surface-verification adapter only when the caller supplies the authorized exact destination identity and complete frozen evaluation tuple. It cannot create a missing adapter, change shared contracts, or turn product/test-audit output into mutation authority.
- **Why:** One terminal assessment can improve durable guidance without turning every task, papercut look, or read-only audit into a maintenance loop. Keeping closure off learning preserves the worker/assurance boundary.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** Per-task learning, counters, calendar audits, background transcript mining, audit-triggered cleanup, or closure rounds around learning create hidden scheduling and unbounded completion cost. Curator-invented expectations or self-grading manufacture success.
- **Consequences:** Standard/high-consequence learning remains one narrow terminal adapter step after verification and review; normal engineering completion then reaches presentation and terminates without automatic audit. Compact and separately requested portfolio audits remain non-mutating with respect to learning. Exact-ID settlement cannot close unrelated records.
- **Reopen when:** Portable assess/review/deep eligibility, backend ordering, closure role scope, papercut accounting/settlement, proof ownership, restoration, or separate-maintenance authority changes.
### D23 — Focused decision provenance

- **Scope:** generic and stage-specific dev-workflow decision maintenance
- **Decision:** Keep `WORKFLOW.md` concise and current. Add a generic and stage-specific decision-authority map there. Store each durable choice once in the narrowest ACTIVE ADR, using stable ID, Scope, Decision, Why, Rejected alternatives, Consequences, and Reopen trigger. Add ID and scope discovery to `docs/adr/INDEX.md`; D24 follows this rule in focused ADR-0007, D27 in focused ADR-0009, and D28 in focused ADR-0003. Keep superseded or rejected history in ADR status and supersession, not skills, memory, plans, transcripts, or one global decision ledger. A non-authoritative repository evidence ledger cannot own rationale or supersede an ADR. Preserve product-workflow ADR-0005 as separate concurrent authority.
- **Why:** Future iterations get both rationale and rejected paths without duplicating executable contracts.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** Per-skill logs repeat generic choices; WORKFLOW history bloats current guidance; one ever-growing decision ledger blurs ownership; treating papercut evidence as authority or provenance would let observations bypass human decisions and focused ADR ownership.
- **Consequences:** Current behavior stays in skills and WORKFLOW; focused ADR decision units explain why and why not; the index and WORKFLOW map make the narrow owner discoverable. ADR-0007 D24 alone owns current cross-workflow papercut rationale, while its ledger remains evidence only; superseded ADR-0006 remains historical authority.
- **Reopen when:** Canonical workflow authority or ADR storage and supersession changes.
