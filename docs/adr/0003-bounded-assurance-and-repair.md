# Bounded assurance and repair

**Status:** ACTIVE  
**Date:** 2026-08-09  
**Updated:** 2026-08-25
**Decision IDs:** D03, D04, D22

## Scope

This decision governs worker smoke, independent verification boundaries, neutral fan-in, final review, diagnosis entry, blocker aggregation, semantic attempts, the single post-assurance repair allowance, worth-framed standard/high-consequence next-loop authority, proof-ceremony closure, and the final review's complexity lens for the generic engineering workflow. It applies to `dev-implementation`, `dev-diagnosing-bugs`, `dev-verification`, `dev-integration`, `dev-code-review`, `dev-handoff`, and their targeted fixtures. It does not let an assurance role repair implementation, authorize a lifecycle reset, create a second review stage, or imply shipping.

## Context / problem

Independent proof is necessary, but proof after every small task can cost more than the feedback it provides and can turn ceremony into the convergence signal. The opposite extreme—waiting until the end while combining unverified isolated lineages—allows defects and identity drift into fan-in. Revisions create a loophole if each verifier or reviewer blocker can restart an unlimited repair/reproof/review cycle. The workflow therefore needs cheap local feedback at each effect, fresh independent evidence at consumable boundaries, one final whole-scope standards/specification review, and a run-wide stop condition that survives derivative revision changes. Simplicity concerns must fit that one final review rather than create duplicate assurance or replace correctness.

## Decisions

### D03 — Post-assurance repair

- **Scope:** Blocker aggregation, semantic attempts, transport retries, the inherited same-outcome repair allowance, finding-lineage identity, original-initial discovery, later-slot closure and impact review, same-outcome repair admission, exhausted standard/high-consequence human checkpoints, otherwise-eligible post-2/2 same-outcome authority asks, worth classification and framing, Continue, Second opinion, terminal Close, grant-scoped cycles, original-review budget precedence, impacted reruns, and terminal convergence.
- **Decision:** Allow one initial semantic attempt and at most one retry for every unchanged Task Contract revision, including high-consequence work and the original repair Task Contract. Attempt 2 is eligible only from attempt-1 evidence of criterion progress, exact blocker resolution, or an authorized materially changed falsifiable hypothesis. A `blocker-resolved` return is consumable only when the Task Contract and Common Handoff map each stable blocker or finding ID to its affected `AC-...`, exact target/caller/failure path, impacted proof recipe, expected result, and observed result on the repaired identity. A universal changed invariant additionally binds a finite current consumer/callsite map and proves every entry. Generic passing suites, changed fixtures, prose assertions, unchanged-hypothesis retries, and a human grant are not progress. Attempt 3 on the same ungranted revision is forbidden, derivative revisions inherit consumed attempts, and two safe idempotent transport retries remain. Collect every exact blocking verification criterion and eligible review finding ID in one assurance pass, then allow one consolidated derivative post-assurance repair revision for the same approved outcome. The run-wide token and original initial-review/rerun counters remain monotonic across derivative revisions.

  The original-initial review is the one whole-scope discovery pass and seals its finding lineages. A lineage is identified by the violated contract or invariant; trigger and expected/observed predicate; observable consumer or affected parent acceptance criterion; causal boundary; finite current consumer/callsite map when the claim spans consumers; and originating target and evidence identity. Paths are evidence, not lineage identity. An original-rerun or grant-scoped pass binds the prior review receipt, remaining lineages, exact repair delta, accepted review impact map, affected and unchanged surfaces, and required finite consumers. It freshly reviews closure of every remaining lineage and every impacted surface; it may reuse original review evidence only for byte-, authority-, contract-, and dependency-identical unaffected surfaces. Verifier receipts are inputs, never the review verdict. Aggregate `APPROVED` requires every prior lineage closed, no repair-caused blocker, no disjoint outcome-relevant blocker, and valid unchanged-surface reuse.

  Finding classification, lineage, and same-outcome repair admission are separate. After original-initial review, same-outcome repair admits only incomplete closure of an existing lineage or a repair-caused new lineage proved by the exact repaired revision, exact changed bytes or contract delta, an accepted D04 impact-map edge, an observable failure path from that delta, and fresh affected proof. A grant or changed hypothesis is authority, not causal evidence. A disjoint non-outcome observation is a terminal advisory. Independently serious safety returns separate-authority intake and never consumes the parent repair set. A disjoint outcome-relevant non-safety defect remains `CHANGES REQUIRED`, keeps the parent incomplete, and returns `authority-change-required` to the outcome authority without silent repair, verification restart, learning, approval, or completion.

  After the current evidence pass, standard/high-consequence work may reach a next-loop human authority question only when the existing exhausted-token/original-review-rerun checkpoint is otherwise eligible, or before another same-outcome authority revision after an exhausted 2/2 revision when the next receiver would otherwise be human authority. Reaching 2/2 alone remains terminal and creates no question. First run the current authority, exact-target, safety, and conclusive-evidence hard-stop gates; missing or stale authority/target identity, unsafe or uncertain effects, or contradictory or inconclusive evidence emits no classification, frame, or question. After those gates pass, classify the stable remaining IDs as `outcome-blocking` only with affirmative evidence that at least one ID prevents the exact delivered artifact from satisfying its parent `OUT-...`; classify them as `proof-ceremony` only with a current exact target, affirmative existing evidence that the delivered artifact satisfies that outcome, and remaining IDs limited to harness, proof-recipe, or evidence-accounting obligations with no causal path to artifact behavior. For `outcome-blocking` only, a repeated frontier, unchanged hypothesis, exhausted attempts without a falsifiable changed approach, or other `no-progress-stop` is a continuation-only gate that stops before presentation and cannot be waived by a human action. Proof-ceremony may frame hypothesis `none`; those continuation-only gates do not suppress its worth frame, Second opinion, or eligible Close, while Continue remains rejected without mutation when no changed hypothesis exists.

  Before every eligible question, the current owner emits exactly five presentation lines in this order: `remaining stable IDs`, `relation to OUT-...`, `what already satisfies the goal`, `changed falsifiable hypothesis`, and `recommendation`. Relation is exactly `outcome-blocking` or `proof-ceremony`; the hypothesis is one materially changed falsifiable approach or `none`; recommendation is exactly `continue-differently`, `independent check`, or `close with residual`. The owner then shows exactly **Continue**, **Second opinion**, and **Close**. Continue grants only one same-owner, same-plan cycle or revision named by the changed hypothesis and remains authority rather than progress; it stays visible but is rejected without mutation when the hypothesis is `none`. At the D03 checkpoint, persist the unchanged eight-line record before the frame. A post-2/2 authority ask creates no record and carries its selected action only once in the Common Handoff and resulting same-plan authority revision; Continue never creates attempt 3 on the exhausted revision.

  Human-selected Second opinion remains optional and never dispatches automatically. For `proof-ceremony`, it uses at most one fresh read-only worker context whose sole objective is whether another loop is worth doing and whose Handoff returns exactly `same-route`, `authority-change`, or `no-progress`; it dispatches no verification, review, or diagnosis role and does not load `rethink` unless the human explicitly selects that advisory method. For `outcome-blocking`, current exact-target verifier, reviewer, or diagnosis eligibility, reuse-first ordering, and duplicate-opinion prohibition remain unchanged. A `same-route` disposition may start only the already-granted cycle and only when the frame names a changed hypothesis; the other dispositions start none.

  Close is accepted only for `proof-ceremony`. It starts no repair or opinion cycle, creates no ad hoc assurance, and mutates neither grant nor opinion. When normal completion gates already pass, it completes with the exact target, parent-outcome satisfaction evidence, and proof-only remaining IDs recorded as terminal residual risk. Otherwise it authorizes only no-call re-accounting of named sealed evidence on the unchanged target; after re-accounting, only the already-scheduled route tail may finish once when normal completion still requires it. Close uses normal `complete` and plan `DONE`, never plan `CLOSED`, backend `cancelled`, unverified-failure wording, a new lifecycle state, or `grant: close`. Ineligible Close is rejected with the exact stable IDs and reason and changes no plan, runtime, record, completion, proof, or cycle state.

  The backend writes one exact eight-line record—exhausted kind/time, trying, found, tried, target, remaining, pending grant, absent opinion—to the executing plan's same authoritative file before asking or resuming. It nests the record under the open task for an ordinary plan or appends it in `Blockers and recovery` for an implementation-grade plan and emits the same bytes inline. No extra fields are allowed. Only the grant line may transition once to timestamped Continue or Second opinion; only the opinion line may then transition once to source/Handoff, one disposition, recommendation, inspected target, and record identity. Older records remain immutable. The newest record alone controls readiness and fresh-session recovery; pending, stale, or opinion-incomplete state blocks, `same-route` may ready the granted same owner, and `authority-change` or `no-progress` remains terminal for that grant.
- **Why:** The inherited token and original review slots still bound automatic work. A compact persisted checkpoint plus one explicit action preserves human control and same-outcome identity, while the required worth frame makes the current owner establish whether another loop can change the delivered outcome. Residual-only Close prevents already-satisfied artifact goals from being held open solely by proof accounting without weakening hard stops, exact-target assurance, or normal completion.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** Attempt 3 from a stray grant, unchanged-hypothesis retry, generic green suite, reset on derivative revision, automatic recursion, reuse of an older grant, or a successor plan solely to regain budget makes convergence depend on activity instead of closed evidence. An unconditional or automatic second opinion, duplicate review, restored original counter, new recovery task/skill/stage/outcome, second ledger, review-owned state, provider-specific policy, or `grant: close` duplicates existing owners or transport. Close on outcome-blocking or inconclusive work hides unfinished behavior. Repeated explicit grants remain eligible only after a separate later eligible exhaustion and a new worth frame; they are not automatic or unbounded revision recursion.
- **Consequences:** Every original Task Contract revision and each grant-bound repair cycle stops at 2/2. Blocking evidence is consolidated before the inherited repair; each claimed resolution remains traceable to the repaired identity and every affected finite consumer. Original review budget is consumed before any grant-scoped pass. No-progress output, repeated evidence, another audit, elapsed time, review count, grant text, opinion, revision, or agent activity is progress. Compact still terminates at its existing bound without a frame, opinion, or Close. Eligible standard/high-consequence next-loop authority is preceded by the five-line worth frame and exactly three actions; post-2/2 asks create no second record. Outcome-blocking work cannot Close. Proof-ceremony Close completes or re-accounts existing proof on the unchanged target with explicit residual risk and no additional cycle.
- **Reopen when:** Blocker-closure fields, finite-consumer proof, semantic-attempt or transport-retry ceilings, inherited token semantics, checkpoint eligibility, worth classification or frame fields, post-2/2 authority asks, Continue or Close eligibility, grant scope, same-plan identity, opinion role or dispositions, original-review precedence, impacted-rerun policy, record grammar, or terminal no-progress conditions change.

### D04 — Assurance boundaries

- **Scope:** Worker smoke, profile-required independent lineage and final verification, neutral fan-in, final whole-scope review, diagnosis entry, and assurance-role neutrality.
- **Decision:** Smoke every implementation task by exercising the changed path or smallest applicable scenario before Handoff. For compact, map every owned acceptance criterion to one deterministic exact-target smoke scenario and require complete expected/observed evidence; criterion-complete worker smoke is terminal proof. Any criterion that requires an independent proof class disqualifies compact before completion. For standard and high-consequence, use fresh read-only verification at every consumable isolated lineage before fan-in, at the final integrated target after neutral fan-in, at the final single-lineage target, and at explicit approved high-consequence checkpoints. A universal changed invariant cannot reach `VERIFIED` until a finite current consumer/callsite map is bound and every entry is proved. Fan in multiple isolated lineages only after each exact lineage is independently verified, consume every named input without semantic winner selection, verify the combined target, and skip integration for one lineage. Run a separate final Standards and Specification review only when the selected assurance profile requires review. Keep diagnosis narrow: one hard unexplained reproducible defect with settled expected behavior may yield a fix contract, blocker, or architecture finding, while routine and known-cause implementation, verification, or review defects repair directly under available authority. Assurance roles never repair implementation in place or choose product/architecture semantics. For same-outcome noncompact repair, freeze the parent acceptance IDs and proof recipes; a complete causal impact map marks every pre-existing criterion impacted or unaffected, reruns impacted proof fresh, and computes the repaired target's aggregate verdict over that unchanged parent set. Reuse unaffected evidence only when the map proves no causal path from the repair and the criterion's target surface, environment, expectation, proof method, fixture and dependency identities, and evidence integrity remain valid; otherwise rerun it fresh.
- **Decision:** Worker smoke exercises the frozen acceptance cases, fixtures, oracles, and every bound finite current consumer/callsite entry and blocks before assurance on a mismatch. Smoke and worker conclusions never become independent verifier evidence. For same-outcome noncompact repair, the repair owner proposes the complete causal impact map, the backend freezes one action for every parent criterion, and the verifier independently accepts or rejects every fresh-proof or unaffected-reuse action before emitting a fresh aggregate verdict. Verification freshness is current target and applicable-rule manifest equality, fresh impacted proof, and that fresh aggregate verdict; unaffected evidence remains reusable only while target surface, environment, expectation, proof method, fixture, dependency, and evidence-integrity identities are exact.
- **Decision:** For standard and high-consequence work, an exact numbered final `dev-verification` → `dev-code-review` → `dev-continual-learning` suffix is an optional human projection of the existing profile tail. When present, consume those rows once; when absent, the backend schedules the same boundaries once. The route-visible `dev-continual-learning` owner invokes portable `assess` exactly once inside its existing task after current once-bound target and applicable-project-rule manifests and the selected role slot validate. Pause or compaction preserves completed boundaries and monotonic slot/counter identities through the Common Handoff; an exact duplicate dispatch tuple fails before dispatch without consuming the slot. Every earlier D04 lineage-verification and neutral-integration boundary remains explicit, and neither plan shape adds or removes a proof pass.
- **Decision:** Bind every criterion to a canonical `surface-proof-recipe/v1` identity before readiness. A non-`none` adapter binding names one exact canonical URI and symlink-safe tree digest. Run doctor once at readiness to establish launch, drive, evidence, isolation, and cleanup capability; doctor never satisfies smoke, a criterion, verifier proof, review, or completion. Recanonicalize recipes and rehash adapters before every assurance or completion boundary. Drift returns `INCONCLUSIVE` or blocks without consuming the boundary. Authorized adapter self-modification invalidates earlier proof and requires final re-drive, tree rehash, and recipe rebind. Compact uses the same complete binding without adding an independent tail, and adapter presence changes no profile or role-independence rule.
- **Why:** Criterion-complete smoke directly proves bounded deterministic compact work. Fresh independent proof remains necessary where consequence, topology, or proof class disqualifies compact, and complete finite-consumer evidence prevents universal claims from hiding an unproved caller. Freezing the parent's acceptance and proof-recipe identities prevents a repair observation from expanding the outcome, while causal impact selection preserves valid proof without hiding changed dependencies.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** Independent verification and review after every eligible compact task replace direct worker proof with ceremony. Final-only proof with unverified isolated fan-in inputs combines untrusted identities. A generic passing suite cannot prove every consumer of a changed invariant. Assurance roles repairing in place destroy independent evidence. Routine failures entering diagnosis or repeated diagnosis under an unchanged hypothesis add a process loop.
- **Consequences:** Compact completes after exact criterion smoke with no verification or review dispatch. Noncompact worker smoke stays immediate while required independent proof remains fresh and read-only. Every fan-in input and combined target has an exact verified identity, and every universal changed invariant has complete finite-consumer proof. Same-outcome repair emits a complete every-criterion impact map, fresh impacted proof, identity-validated unaffected reuse, and one aggregate verdict over the unchanged parent set. Assurance profile remains immutable and independent of lifecycle depth and topology.
- **Reopen when:** Compact terminal proof, disqualifiers, finite-consumer proof, independent lineage/final/high-consequence boundaries, fan-in prerequisites or neutrality, final review applicability, diagnosis entry, role neutrality, immutable identity, fixed parent acceptance/proof identity, impact-map selection, evidence-reuse validity, or assurance independence changes.

### D22 — Complexity lens inside the final review

- **Scope:** `dev-code-review` Standards and Review Handoff, finding classification and lineage, original-initial whole-scope discovery, and later original-rerun or grant-scoped closure and impact review when the selected assurance profile requires review.
- **Decision:** Keep one final `dev-code-review` pass for standard and high-consequence work and retain the `delete`, `reuse`, `stdlib`, `native`, `yagni`, and `shrink` Standards tags. A same-outcome blocking finding requires direct behavioral or direct static evidence that an existing parent AC-... or an observable changed-contract consumer is broken. Changed paths, prose, metadata, frontmatter, scanner-string equality, stale adjacent explanation, and self-referential consistency assertions alone are advisory. When contradictory current governing authority makes expected behavior indeterminate, return INCONCLUSIVE to the authority owner without classifying a blocker or advisory, authorizing repair, or completing. No-effect proof compares the declared causal pre/post boundary and included targets; later changes to explicitly excluded mutable files do not invalidate it. Unrelated pre-existing defects, unrelated dirty bytes, mutable sidecar drift, and unsupported suspicion are advisory or deferred. An independently serious safety issue returns a separate authority stop or intake and never silently enters the same repair set. A wording-only advisory is terminal residual risk: it does not fail the parent or restart verification, review, or learning, and the parent performs its one already-required terminal Standard assessment before completion. If cleanup is later elected, dev-ask classifies a new maintenance outcome with fresh authority, acceptance, Task Contract, target, attempts, and assurance; no parent repair, verification, review, or learning state is inherited. Carry results in the existing Review Handoff; add no review stage, second Handoff, metric, maintenance dispatch, or shipping wording.
- **Decision:** Original-initial review seals the initial finding lineages. An original-rerun or grant-scoped pass is not another whole-scope discovery pass: it binds the prior receipt, remaining lineages, exact repair delta, accepted review impact map, affected and unchanged surfaces, and finite consumers; freshly reviews remaining lineages and impacted surfaces; and reuses original evidence only for byte-, authority-, contract-, and dependency-identical unaffected surfaces. D22 classification remains unchanged. Incomplete existing lineages and directly proved repair-caused regressions are same-outcome repair-eligible; grant-hypothesis-only admission is forbidden; disjoint non-outcome observations are terminal advisories; independently serious safety is separate intake; and disjoint outcome-relevant non-safety defects remain `CHANGES REQUIRED` but return `authority-change-required` instead of silently entering another same-outcome repair cycle. Aggregate approval requires all prior lineages closed, no repair-caused or disjoint outcome-relevant blocker, and valid unchanged-surface reuse; verifier receipts are evidence inputs, not the verdict.
- **Decision:** A numbered `dev-code-review` row consumes the one existing final review; when that row is absent, the backend schedules the same review once. Never execute both forms or add a second review.
- **Decision:** Final review rechecks the verifier-bound recipe and adapter identities and treats doctor receipts as readiness provenance only. Adapter presence changes no review eligibility, slot, scope, independence, verdict policy, or repair budget.
- **Why:** One broad initial pass preserves credible discovery. Predicate-bound lineages and bounded closure/impact passes prevent later slots from rebuilding valid review evidence or silently expanding same-outcome authority, while unchanged D22 classification and direct causal admission keep real outcome defects blocking.
- **Rejected alternatives / why not:** Reviewing compact after criterion-complete smoke adds a disallowed assurance tail. A standalone or complexity-only review violates the one-review invariant. Treating every observed defect or mutable-file drift as a same-outcome blocker silently expands authority. Net lines lack a repeatable method; `Ship` conflicts with separate delivery authority.
- **Consequences:** Profile-required original-initial review performs one whole-scope pass with a named simplicity lens and seals its lineages. A later eligible slot closes those lineages and reviews repair impact without becoming a fresh broad audit. Only outcome-relevant findings with direct evidence of a broken parent criterion or observable changed-contract consumer are blockers; a consumer-only blocker maps to `affected AC: none` plus its fixed contract/consumer without becoming a criterion. Incomplete same-lineage findings and causally proved repair regressions may enter the bounded repair set. Disjoint outcome-relevant non-safety findings keep the parent incomplete and return to outcome authority; terminal advisories continue the one already-scheduled learning assessment through the route-visible `dev-continual-learning` adapter's single portable `assess` invocation and then completion, without assurance replay. Authority conflicts return `INCONCLUSIVE`, and separate safety intake cannot consume the repair set.
- **Reopen when:** Review applicability, direct-evidence relevance, finding classification, lineage identity, original-initial discovery scope, later-slot closure/impact scope, same-outcome repair admission, aggregate verdict composition, authority-conflict classification, terminal advisory behavior, separate-maintenance ownership, causal no-effect boundaries, the one-final-review invariant, finding policy, or Common Handoff ownership changes.
